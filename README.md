
# Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU)

## Introduction

The **Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU)** is one of my favorite movie franchises. It features superheroes, villains, and exciting stories connected across different movies.

I enjoy the MCU because of its *storytelling, action, characters, and interconnected movies.*

---

## My Favorite Characters

Here are my favorite Marvel characters:

- **Spider-Man** – A friendly neighborhood superhero.
- **Iron Man** – A genius inventor and superhero.
- **Doctor Doom** – One of Marvel's most interesting villains.

## Why I Like the MCU

I enjoy watching Marvel movies because:

1. The stories are entertaining.
2. The characters have interesting backgrounds.
3. The movies are connected to one another.
4. The villains make the stories more exciting.
